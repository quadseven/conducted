HOW TO PLAY

  • A red freight car is loose on the loop and picking up speed. Drive your green engine up behind it, match its speed, and touch gently to couple. Then hold brake until both stop.
  • Passenger trains cross the loop at gates A, B and C. Close a gate (red) and they wait at the signal. A runaway in a crossing with a passenger train ends the game.
  • Points 1, 2 and 3 steer trains. Points 3 can send the runaway into the sand drag siding, which stops it for a smaller bonus. Hit any buffer stop too fast and it is a wreck.
  • Passenger trains that leave on time score 100. Keep gates closed too long and they run late.

Keyboard: Up/W power, Down/S brake, Space next points ahead, 1 2 3 points, A B C gates, R reverse, P pause, M mute.

Touch: tap a gate or set of points on the map, or use the buttons.
