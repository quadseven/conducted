# Steamspring Village map layout

The existing story design gives Steamspring a Train Museum as its story hub,
but it does not name residents or specify how many homes the village has. The
map therefore reserves two additional unnamed home entrances rather than
inventing residents or plot. In game, the houses are labeled West House and
East House.

- West House occupies a 3 by 3 house footprint at (12, 7).
- East House occupies a 3 by 3 house footprint at (16, 7).
- Both entrances lead to empty rooms with a return mat. They have no NPCs,
  dialogue, services, trainers, or story events.
- The east/west names are map labels, not claims about village residents.
  Future story work may assign owners and replace room interiors without moving
  the gym, civic buildings, or route connections.

This is a map reservation only. It does not assign either home to a character
or make a claim about village lore.
